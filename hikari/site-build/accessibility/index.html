Design approach

The pages use landmarks, a skip link, readable contrast, descriptive link text and a mobile navigation control. Decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.

Known limitations

Some external websites linked from this site may not share the same accessibility features. A full assistive-technology review has not been completed.
