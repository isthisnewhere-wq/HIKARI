Study planning

Choose a school and course that fit your aims.

Start by identifying your intended level, subject, language of instruction and possible start date. Compare information directly from schools and the official Study in Japan portal. Requirements differ by programme and institution.

HIKARI lists Japan as a destination it handles. What support it offers with school selection or applications has not been confirmed; ask before assuming a service is included.

Keep sources in their lane

Education and immigration are separate checks.

Use the school’s official pages for admission. Use Japan’s official visa information and the Embassy of Japan in Nepal for visa procedures.

Quick answers

Before you take the next step

Where can I check Japanese study options?

Use the official Study in Japan website and the official pages of individual institutions.

Where should I check visa information in Nepal?

Review the Embassy of Japan in Nepal’s visa information page and follow its current instructions.

Can HIKARI promise a place or visa?

No. Schools make admission decisions and the relevant Japanese authorities make visa decisions.
